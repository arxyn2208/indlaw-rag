Meera Das v Harbor Education Board (fictional case, not real law)
1. The appellant was denied admission to a college because the Board applied a cut-off date that was announced after applications had closed.
2. The Board contended that it had full discretion in setting cut-off dates.
3. The Court held that a rule changed after the application stage cannot be applied against candidates who applied under the earlier rule.
4. The Court observed that discretion must be exercised reasonably and with notice to those affected.
5. The Board was directed to consider the appellant for admission under the original cut-off.
