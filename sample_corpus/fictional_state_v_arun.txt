State of Northvale v Arun Pillai (fictional case, not real law)
1. The accused was arrested on a Monday evening and produced before a magistrate on Thursday morning.
2. The accused argued that his detention beyond twenty four hours without production before a magistrate was illegal.
3. The Court held that production before a magistrate within twenty four hours of arrest, excluding travel time, is mandatory.
4. The Court held that the accused was entitled to be released on bail because of the delay, although the trial would continue.
